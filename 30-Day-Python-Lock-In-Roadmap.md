# 🎯 30-Day Rules

1. **No zero days.**
2. Code every day.
3. Don't copy solutions immediately.
4. Try solving problems yourself first.
5. Ask AI for explanations, not just answers.
6. Build projects instead of only watching tutorials.
7. Push your work to GitHub.
8. Track your progress every day.
9. Compete with your bro, but compete against yesterday's version of yourself too.
10. **Consistency > motivation.**
